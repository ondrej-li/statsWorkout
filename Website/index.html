What it does

Any period you need

Rolling 24 hours, a single day, calendar week, month, quarter, year or a custom range — with the same five metrics in every view.

Five metrics, one place

All-day steps, workout distance, elevation gain, exercise time and the number of activities, each with a total and an average.

Fair comparisons

Every period is compared with the previous one, or with the same period last year. A month in progress is only compared with the same elapsed days.

Charts you can touch

Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and tap a bar or the progress line to read the exact value for that hour, day, week or month.

Your units

Kilometres or miles, metres or feet — switch once and every number and axis follows.

Private by design

Read-only access to Apple Health, all maths on the device, no account, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no server.
